Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

SafeBite ("the app") helps you check whether a food product matches your personal dietary needs. This page explains what data the app touches and where it goes.

What's stored, and where

Your dietary profile, connections list, and recently-checked products are stored in your browser's local storage, on your own device. We don't operate accounts, logins, or a central database of app users.

BiteID sharing

If you create a BiteID and share it with someone, your dietary-preference fields (allergies, diets, avoided ingredients/foods, nutrition limits) are stored on our server under that code so people you share it with can look them up. No name, email, or other identifying information is stored alongside it — the code itself is the only "key." Treat your BiteID like a link you're sharing: anyone who has it can read that profile's food preferences.

Third-party services this app talks to

  • Open Food Facts — product and ingredient data for barcode scans and search. See their privacy policy.
  • U.S. FDA (openFDA) — public food-recall data, checked against your own recently-viewed products.
  • Google AdSense (if enabled) — may show ads in the app. Google may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or generally at aboutads.info. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on someone's prior visits to this app or other sites on the internet.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data. We don't require an account. We don't track you across other websites ourselves — any cross-site tracking would come only from the third-party ad network above, if and when ads are active.

Your choices

You can clear your profile, connections, and history at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings, or by using the in-app controls to remove individual connections or reset your preferences.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the app's developer/publisher directly.

This policy is a general template covering the app's actual data flows and is not a substitute for legal advice. If you plan to run ads or operate this app commercially, especially for users in the EU/UK (GDPR) or California (CCPA/CPRA), have this reviewed by a professional before relying on it.
