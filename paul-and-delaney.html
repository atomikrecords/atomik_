The Setting

The Backyard Venue

Rustic elegance in the heart of DFW

Tucked into the Dallas–Fort Worth countryside, The Backyard Venue pairs gentle waterfalls, string-lit gardens, and a timber reception barn for a setting that feels equal parts romantic and unforgettable.

From the bridal cottage to the groom's man cave, every corner of the property has been designed for the kind of day that unfolds exactly as it should — relaxed, warm, and beautiful from every angle.

  • Waterfall garden ceremony lawn
  • Timber reception barn & pavilion
  • Bridal cottage & groom's cave
  • Golf cart guest transport
  • Bistro & string lighting throughout
  • Clear-umbrella weather coverage

Suggested Attire

Garden formal / cocktail attire. Think flowing fabrics in sage, navy, dusty blue, and neutral tones — outdoor ceremony, so consider block heels or flats on the lawn.

Please Avoid

All-white or ivory (reserved for the bride) and bright florals that clash with the garden setting. When in doubt, sage, navy, and soft neutrals are always a safe choice.
