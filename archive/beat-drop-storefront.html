Questions

What happens right after I order?

You'll get a confirmation with payment info if you haven't already paid — once payment's in, the beat file and signed license go out the same way you ordered.

How fast is delivery?

Same day for standard tiers once payment clears. Exclusive deals may take a bit longer to finalize paperwork.

Can I get a refund?

Before files are delivered, yes, no questions asked. Once the beat file is sent, all sales are final — that's standard for digital licensing.

Can two people buy a non-exclusive lease on the same beat?

Yes — that's exactly what "non-exclusive" means. If you want it removed from the catalog entirely, that's the Exclusive Rights tier.
